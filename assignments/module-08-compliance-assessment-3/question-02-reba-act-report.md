### 2. Purpose of the legislation / 立法目的

**Question / 题目要求**

What is the purpose of the legislation?

In your own words, explain the purpose of the Act you researched.

用自己的话解释 REBA Act 的立法目的。

**Answer in English / 英文答案草稿**

The purpose of the REBA Act is to regulate and supervise people who work as real estate agents, business agents and sales representatives in Western Australia. It sets licensing, registration, conduct, trust account and disciplinary requirements so that clients and the public are protected and real estate work is carried out lawfully and professionally.

**中文解析**

这里要概括 REBA Act 的监管功能。重点不只是笼统写 "protect consumers"，还要说明它通过 licensing、registration、conduct、trust accounts 和 discipline 等制度来规范 WA 房地产从业者，确保业务合法、专业，并保护客户和公众。

**依据来源**

- `legislation/wa-reba-act-1978.md` - Act long title: it makes provision for the "regulation and supervision" of persons acting in real estate or business transactions.
- WA official legislation page: https://www.legislation.wa.gov.au/legislation/statutes.nsf/law_a672.html

### 3. Key components / 关键组成部分

**Question / 题目要求**

Key components.

List two components of the REBA Act that carry legal obligations or responsibilities with penalties for non-compliance.

列出两个 REBA Act 中带有法律义务或责任、且不合规会有处罚的组成部分。

**Answer in English / 英文答案草稿**

1. Licensing and registration requirements for agents and sales representatives, including the requirement to hold the correct licence, triennial certificate or certificate of registration.

2. Professional conduct obligations, including written appointments, proper handling of client interests, disclosure of conflicts of interest, trust account duties and compliance with the Code of Conduct.

**中文解析**

第一个 component 选 licensing and registration，因为这是 REBA Act 对从业资格的基础要求。第二个 component 选 professional conduct and compliance，因为它直接对应题干要求关注 professional conduct、legal responsibilities 和 compliance expectations。两个点都能连接到具体 section 和 penalty。

**依据来源**

- `legislation/wa-reba-act-1978.md` - s 26 requires real estate and business agents to be licensed and hold a current triennial certificate.
- `legislation/wa-reba-act-1978.md` - s 44 requires real estate sales representatives to hold a current certificate of registration.
- `legislation/wa-reba-act-1978.md` - s 60 requires a valid written appointment before commission can be claimed.
- `legislation/wa-reba-act-1978.md` - s 64 regulates conflicts of interest.
- `materials/module-08-compliance/workbooks/08-wb3-professional-communication-ethical-practice.md` - Section 3.4 explains conflict disclosure and written consent under REBA Act s 64.

### 4. Breach examples / 违规例子

**Question / 题目要求**

Provide two examples of conduct that would be considered a breach, and include the relevant section number from the REBA Act.

提供两个会构成 breach 的行为例子，并写出 REBA Act 的相关 section number。

**Answer in English / 英文答案草稿**

1. A person carries on business, or holds themselves out, as a real estate agent without being licensed and holding a current triennial certificate. This breaches REBA Act s 26(1).

2. An agent or sales representative has a direct or indirect interest in a transaction they are acting in, without the principal's prior written consent. This breaches REBA Act s 64(1) or s 64(2).

**中文解析**

这两个例子适合填表，因为 REBA Act 对它们都有明确 section number 和 penalty。第一个是无牌经营或冒充/表示自己是 agent，属于资格合规红线。第二个是利益冲突，和 Workbook 3 的 ethics and compliance 内容直接相关。

**依据来源**

- `legislation/wa-reba-act-1978.md` - s 26(1), unlicensed real estate or business agency work.
- `legislation/wa-reba-act-1978.md` - s 64(1) and s 64(2), conflicts of interest without prior written consent.
- `materials/module-08-compliance/workbooks/08-wb3-professional-communication-ethical-practice.md` - Section 3.4 states that conflicts require written disclosure and written client consent.

### 5. Penalties / 处罚

**Question / 题目要求**

For each breach you have listed, provide the penalty, including the dollar amount, that applies.

为上面列出的每个 breach 写出适用处罚，包括具体 dollar amount。

**Answer in English / 英文答案草稿**

1. Breach of s 26(1): fine of $100,000.

2. Breach of s 64(1) or s 64(2): fine of $5,000. The court may also order the person to pay any profit from the transaction to the principal.

**中文解析**

这里不要使用 general penalty，因为 s 26 和 s 64 自身已经列明具体罚款。s 64 还规定，法院可以命令违规者把从该交易中已经或可能获得的 profit 支付给 principal，所以可作为补充后果写入答案。

**依据来源**

- `legislation/wa-reba-act-1978.md` - s 26(1) penalty: fine of $100,000.
- `legislation/wa-reba-act-1978.md` - s 64(1) and s 64(2) penalty: fine of $5,000.
- `legislation/wa-reba-act-1978.md` - s 64(3), court may order profit to be paid to the principal.
