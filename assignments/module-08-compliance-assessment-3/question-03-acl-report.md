### 2. Purpose of the legislation / 立法目的

**Question / 题目要求**

What is the purpose of the legislation?

In your own words, explain the purpose of the Act you researched.

用自己的话解释该 Act / ACL 的目的。

**Answer in English / 英文答案草稿**

The purpose of the Competition and Consumer Act 2010 and the ACL is to promote fair trading, protect consumers, and prevent businesses from engaging in unfair, misleading or deceptive conduct. In real estate practice, it requires agents and representatives to advertise honestly, give accurate information, respect consumer rights, and follow rules for consumer agreements such as door knocking or cold calling.

**中文解析**

这里要把 ACL 和房地产实务联系起来。房地产从业者在 advertising、listing presentation、property information、door knocking 和 cold calling 中都不能误导消费者，也要遵守 unsolicited consumer agreement 的规则。

**依据来源**

- Competition and Consumer Act 2010 (Cth) s 2 - object is to promote competition, fair trading and consumer protection.
- `materials/module-08-compliance/workbooks/08-wb3-professional-communication-ethical-practice.md` - Section 4.4 explains ACL rules for door knocking and unsolicited consumer contracts.

### 3. Key components / 关键组成部分

**Question / 题目要求**

Key components.

List two components of the Competition and Consumer Act 2010 that carry legal obligations or responsibilities with penalties for non-compliance.

列出两个带有法律义务或责任、且不合规会有处罚的组成部分。

**Answer in English / 英文答案草稿**

1. False or misleading representations, especially in advertising, marketing, property descriptions, price statements and representations about land.

2. Unsolicited consumer agreement rules, including door knocking or cold calling requirements, written agreement rules, cooling-off rights, and restrictions on taking payment or starting services during the cooling-off period.

**中文解析**

第一个 component 直接对应房地产广告和销售陈述。第二个 component 对应 Workbook 3 的 door knocking case study，课程明确提到不能当场收钱、不能在 10 business day cooling-off period 内开始服务。

**依据来源**

- Competition and Consumer Act 2010 (Cth) sch 2 ss 29, 30; offence equivalents ss 151, 152.
- Competition and Consumer Act 2010 (Cth) sch 2 ss 69, 79, 86, 179.
- `materials/module-08-compliance/workbooks/08-wb3-professional-communication-ethical-practice.md` - Section 4.4.

### 4. Breach examples / 违规例子

**Question / 题目要求**

Provide two examples of conduct that would be considered a breach, and include the relevant section number from the Competition and Consumer Act 2010.

提供两个会构成 breach 的行为例子，并写出 Competition and Consumer Act 2010 的相关 section number。

**Answer in English / 英文答案草稿**

1. A real estate representative makes a false or misleading statement about the price, location, characteristics, permitted use, or facilities of a property being sold. This breaches ACL s 152(1), in Schedule 2 of the Competition and Consumer Act 2010.

2. A representative obtains a listing through door knocking, then accepts advertising money or starts marketing services during the 10 business day cooling-off period. This breaches ACL s 179(1), in Schedule 2 of the Competition and Consumer Act 2010.

**中文解析**

第一个例子适合房地产场景，因为 ACL s 152 专门针对 sale or grant of an interest in land 的 false or misleading representations。第二个例子来自 Workbook 3 case study：door knocking 后当场收取 advertising cheque 并开始 marketing，违反 unsolicited consumer agreement 规则。

**依据来源**

- Competition and Consumer Act 2010 (Cth) sch 2 s 152(1).
- Competition and Consumer Act 2010 (Cth) sch 2 s 179(1).
- `materials/module-08-compliance/workbooks/08-wb3-professional-communication-ethical-practice.md` - Section 4.4 case study.

### 5. Penalties / 处罚

**Question / 题目要求**

For each breach you have listed, provide the penalty, including the dollar amount, that applies.

为上面列出的每个 breach 写出适用处罚，包括具体 dollar amount。

**Answer in English / 英文答案草稿**

1. Breach of ACL s 152(1): for a body corporate, a fine of up to the greater of $100,000,000, three times the value of the benefit, or 30% of adjusted turnover. For an individual, a fine of up to $2,500,000.

2. Breach of ACL s 179(1): $50,000 for a body corporate, or $10,000 for an individual.

**中文解析**

s 152 是高额 false or misleading representation offence。官方 2026 最新汇编版本显示 body corporate 最高基准金额为 $100,000,000，individual 为 $2,500,000。s 179 是 unsolicited consumer agreement 的具体 offence，罚款为 body corporate $50,000，individual $10,000。

**依据来源**

- Competition and Consumer Act 2010 (Cth) sch 2 s 152 penalty.
- Competition and Consumer Act 2010 (Cth) sch 2 s 179 penalty.
- Federal Register latest version: https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A00109/latest
