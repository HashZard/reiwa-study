# Compliance Cluster - Assessment 3

## Question 4 / 题目 4

### English

As a real estate professional at Phantom Realty, you are legally required to stay up to date with current legislation and be able to recognise and respond to legal discrepancies in your day-to-day work.

The table below lists five key strategy areas.

For each one, complete the "How" and "Why" columns to explain:

- actions you will take (How)
- reasons these actions are important for compliance (Why).

Your responses should be clear, practical, and show how you will maintain ongoing legal awareness in your role.

### 中文题意

你作为 Phantom Realty 的房地产从业者，需要保持对现行法律的了解，并能在日常工作中识别和处理法律差异或不一致。题目给出五个策略领域，要求分别填写：

- How：你会采取什么行动
- Why：这些行动为什么对合规重要

答案要实用、清楚，并体现你会持续维护法律意识。

## Answer / 作答

### 1. Accessing legislation / 获取法规

**Question / 题目要求**

Accessing legislation - explain how you will access current legislation and why this is necessary.

说明你如何查阅现行法规，以及为什么这样做对合规重要。

**Answer in English / 英文答案草稿**

**How:** I will use official legislation websites, such as WA legislation and the Federal Register of Legislation, to check current Acts, regulations and codes before relying on legal information. I will save links to key laws used in my role, such as the REBA Act, the Code of Conduct and the ACL.

**Why:** This helps me rely on current and accurate legal sources, not memory or outdated notes. It reduces the risk of giving wrong advice, using old rules, or missing a legal requirement in agency work.

**中文解析**

这里重点是 “current legislation”。不要只写 Google 搜索；应强调 official legislation websites 和保存常用法规链接。这样能证明你知道资料来源要权威、现行。

- `materials/module-08-compliance/workbooks/08-wb3-professional-communication-ethical-practice.md` - Section 4 Overview requires representatives to stay up to date with legislation, regulations and codes, and know where to find credible legal, ethical and compliance advice.
- `materials/module-08-compliance/workbooks/08-wb3-professional-communication-ethical-practice.md` - Section 4.3 says agencies provide updates but representatives also have a personal duty to stay informed.

### 2. Understanding legal principles / 理解法律原则

**Question / 题目要求**

Understanding legal principles - explain how you will understand legal principles and why this is necessary.

说明你如何理解法律原则，以及为什么这对合规重要。

**Answer in English / 英文答案草稿**

**How:** I will read agency guidance, training notes and regulator updates, and ask my licensee or manager if I am unsure how a rule applies. I will use the compliance hierarchy: law first, then best practice, then personal ethics.

**Why:** Understanding the principle behind a rule helps me apply it correctly in real situations. It also stops me from relying only on good intentions when the law requires a specific process.

**中文解析**

这里要体现不是机械背条文，而是理解 rule 的 practical application。WB3 明确说 good intentions are not enough，并要求按照 compliance hierarchy：law first, best practice, personal ethics。

**依据来源**

- `materials/module-08-compliance/workbooks/08-wb3-professional-communication-ethical-practice.md` - Section 4.2 states that good intentions are not enough and technical knowledge is needed.

### 3. Addressing discrepancies / 处理法律差异或不一致

**Question / 题目要求**

Addressing discrepancies - explain how you will respond when you notice a legal discrepancy.

说明你发现法律、政策、文件或实际做法不一致时会如何处理，以及为什么重要。

**Answer in English / 英文答案草稿**

**How:** If I notice a discrepancy between a form, agency process, client instruction or current law, I will stop and check the issue before acting. I will document the concern, compare it with the current legislation or agency policy, and escalate it to my licensee or manager.

**Why:** This prevents small errors from becoming compliance breaches. It also protects the client, the agency and my registration by making sure legal issues are handled by the right person before work continues.

**中文解析**

这里要写 “stop, check, document, escalate”。WB3 多次强调 serious/legal issues 要升级给 licensee 或 agent-in-charge。题目要求 recognise and respond to legal discrepancies，所以答案要体现发现问题后不能自己硬处理。

**依据来源**

- `materials/module-08-compliance/workbooks/08-wb3-professional-communication-ethical-practice.md` - Section 1 notes serious, legal or unresolved complaints should be escalated to the licensee or agent-in-charge.
- `materials/module-08-compliance/workbooks/08-wb3-professional-communication-ethical-practice.md` - Section 4.1 says to follow agency policies and think critically about risk and compliance in daily work.

### 4. Maintaining your knowledge base / 维护知识库

**Question / 题目要求**

Maintaining your knowledge base - explain how you will keep your knowledge up to date.

说明你会如何持续维护自己的知识库，以及为什么这样做重要。

**Answer in English / 英文答案草稿**

**How:** I will keep a simple compliance folder with key legislation links, agency procedures, regulator updates, CPD notes and common checklists. I will review it regularly and update it after training, team meetings or changes to agency policy.

**Why:** A current knowledge base gives me a reliable place to check information before making decisions. It supports consistent work and helps me avoid repeating mistakes in high-risk areas such as disclosure, trust money and safety.

**中文解析**

这里强调 practical system：compliance folder、checklists、CPD notes、regular review。WB3 提到要参加 CPD、阅读 agency communications、关注 high-risk areas，并持续学习。

**依据来源**

- `materials/module-08-compliance/workbooks/08-wb3-professional-communication-ethical-practice.md` - Section 4.1 says to pay extra attention to high-risk areas such as disclosure, trust money and safety.
- `materials/module-08-compliance/workbooks/08-wb3-professional-communication-ethical-practice.md` - Section 4.3 lists agency compliance communications, CPD and trusted update sources as ways to stay current.

### 5. Tracking changes and amendments / 跟踪法律变化和修订

**Question / 题目要求**

Tracking changes and amendments - explain how you will track legal changes and amendments.

说明你会如何跟踪法规变化和修订，以及为什么这样做重要。

**Answer in English / 英文答案草稿**

**How:** I will set a monthly reminder to check trusted update sources, including agency bulletins, LGIRS or Consumer Protection updates, Landgate updates and REIWA newsletters if available. I will note any changes that affect my daily tasks and ask for guidance if a change is unclear.

**Why:** Laws, forms and regulator expectations can change. Tracking changes helps me use the current process, avoid outdated practices, and respond quickly when a new rule affects clients or agency files.

**中文解析**

WB3 明确建议 set a calendar reminder 每月检查 trusted sources，并列出 LGIRS、Landgate、REIWA newsletters。这里把它改写为日常行动：monthly reminder、note changes、ask guidance。

**依据来源**

- `materials/module-08-compliance/workbooks/08-wb3-professional-communication-ethical-practice.md` - Section 4.3 recommends reading agency compliance communications, attending CPD and subscribing to trusted sources such as LGIRS, Landgate and REIWA newsletters.
- `materials/module-08-compliance/workbooks/08-wb3-professional-communication-ethical-practice.md` - Section 4.3 recommends setting a calendar reminder to check sources each month.
