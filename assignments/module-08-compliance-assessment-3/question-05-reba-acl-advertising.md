## Question 5 / 题目 5

### English

Explain how the REBA Act and the Australian Consumer Law (ACL) work together in regulating real estate advertising. Why must agents comply with both?

### 中文题意

## Answer / 作答

### 1. How the REBA Act and ACL work together / 两部法律如何共同作用

**Question / 题目要求**

Explain how the REBA Act and ACL work together in regulating real estate advertising.

说明 REBA Act 和 ACL 在房地产广告监管中分别管什么、如何互相配合。

**Answer in English / 英文答案草稿**

The REBA Act and the ACL work together by regulating different parts of real estate advertising. The REBA Act and the Code of Conduct set professional rules for agents and sales representatives. They require advertising to be authorised, linked to the correct agent or agency, consistent with the client’s instructions, and supported by reasonable checks of material facts.

The ACL gives broader consumer protection rules. It prohibits advertising or statements that are misleading, deceptive, or likely to mislead. In real estate, this includes false or misleading claims about the price, location, characteristics, use, or facilities of a property.

Together, the REBA Act focuses on professional conduct and agency obligations, while the ACL focuses on protecting consumers from unfair or misleading advertising.

**中文解析**

这题的关键是不要把两部法律混成一部。REBA Act / REBA Code 主要管房地产从业者的 professional conduct，例如广告必须有授权、必须符合客户指示、必须核实 material facts。ACL 则从 consumer protection 角度管所有 trade or commerce 中的 misleading/deceptive conduct，尤其是房地产广告中关于 land 的价格、位置、特征、用途、设施等陈述。

### 2. Why agents must comply with both / 为什么必须同时遵守

**Question / 题目要求**

Why must agents comply with both?

解释为什么 agents 不能只遵守其中一部法律，而必须同时符合 REBA Act 和 ACL。

**Answer in English / 英文答案草稿**

Agents must comply with both because the same advertisement can create risks under both laws. For example, if an agent advertises a property with an incorrect price, exaggerated features, or missing material facts, the conduct may breach professional obligations under the REBA Act and Code of Conduct, and also breach the ACL as misleading advertising.

Complying with both laws helps protect consumers, sellers, buyers and the agency. It also reduces the risk of complaints, disciplinary action, fines, loss of trust, and damage to the agent’s professional reputation.

**中文解析**

同一个广告行为可能同时触发两套责任。例如广告写错价格、夸大 zoning potential、隐瞒重大缺陷，既可能违反 REBA Code 的授权、material facts、misleading conduct 要求，也可能违反 ACL 的 misleading/deceptive conduct 或 false representation about land。agents 必须同时遵守，是因为 REBA Act 管职业资格和行业纪律，ACL 管消费者保护和市场公平。

- `legislation/cth-australian-consumer-law.md` - ACL ss 18 and 30 apply to misleading conduct and false or misleading representations in trade or commerce.
