# Module 9 — Assignment 1 — Question 1

## Question / 题目 1

### English

You are working at Phantom Realty as a sales representative based in Fantasy Glen. Select three prospecting methods. For each method, identify the target market and how you will promote Phantom Realty’s brand, list three key actions, and set a realistic timeframe or frequency.

### 中文题意

你在 Fantasy Glen 的 Phantom Realty 担任 sales representative。选择三种 prospecting 方法，并分别说明目标市场、如何推广 Phantom Realty 品牌、三项行动步骤，以及实际可行的时间或频率。

## Answer / 作答

### Answer in English / 英文答案草稿

| Prospecting method/strategy | Target market and agency promotion | Three key actions | Timeframe / frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Telephone calls | Past clients, investors and local owners who have previously requested an appraisal. I will introduce myself as a sales representative of Phantom Realty and provide helpful local market information. | 1. Review the CRM and call the contacts due for follow-up. 2. Use a short, polite script and ask about their property plans. 3. Record the enquiry, result and next follow-up in the CRM. | Ten targeted calls each week, during permitted calling times. |
| Door knocking | Owners in my Fantasy Glen farm areas, especially people who may be considering selling or leasing. I will wear professional branding, identify Phantom Realty clearly and offer a local market update. | 1. Plan the streets and prepare information before visiting. 2. Introduce myself, explain the purpose and listen to the owner. 3. Record any enquiry and send a follow-up message only where appropriate. | One farm-area session each week, rotating through the areas. |
| Social media and digital prospecting | Fantasy Glen residents targeted by area, age or interest. I will use consistent Phantom Realty branding, clear property information and honest claims. | 1. Post a useful local market update or appraisal message. 2. Use a targeted Facebook or Instagram campaign. 3. Monitor enquiries and campaign results, then record genuine prospects in the database. | Three posts each week and one targeted campaign each month. |

这份计划选择 phone calls、door knocking 和 social media，分别覆盖现有关系、farm area 屋主和 Fantasy Glen 数字受众。每种方法都有三项行动、目标对象、Phantom Realty 品牌呈现方式和实际频率。
