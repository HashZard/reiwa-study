## Answer / 作答

### Answer in English / 英文答案草稿

Select these three options:

1. **Set calendar reminders to follow up leads**
2. **Log contacts and follow-ups in a CRM system**
3. **Record client feedback in your prospecting report**

### 中文解析

这三项都能形成可追踪的 follow-up 或结果记录。`Use social media likes as your only record` 不正确，因为课程明确要求关注 CRM 中的实际 leads，而不是只看 vanity metrics。
