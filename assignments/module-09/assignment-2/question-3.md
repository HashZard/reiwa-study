# Module 9 — Assignment 2 — Question 3

## Question / 题目 3

### English

While speaking with the client on the phone, what key information would you need to clarify before proceeding with an appraisal?

Provide at least two sources you could use to verify property and ownership details.

### 中文题意

说明电话中开始 appraisal 前要确认的关键信息，并列出至少两个核验物业及产权资料的来源。

## Answer / 作答

### Answer in English / 英文答案草稿

I would clarify the purpose of the appraisal, the property address and legal description, and the client’s identity and authority to act for the owner.

I would use:

1. A title search and Certificate of Title to confirm the registered owner, tenure and encumbrances.
2. Appropriate owner identification, such as a 100-point ID check for an individual registered owner.

### 中文解析

题目同时要求电话中要确认的资料和至少两个核验来源。Title search/Certificate of Title 核验物业与注册业主；100-point ID 核验提出要求者的身份。还须确认其是否有出售或代表出售的授权。
