# Module 9 — Assignment 3 — Question 5

## Question / 题目 5

### English

Under the REBA Act, an agency loses the right to claim commission if:

Options: the property does not sell within 60 days; the agent charges a fixed rate; the appointment to act is invalid or unsigned; the sale price is below market value.

### 中文题意

识别 REBA Act 下代理机构无权收取 commission 的情形。

## Answer / 作答

### Answer in English / 英文答案草稿

The appointment to act is not valid or is not signed by the client.

### 中文解析

REBA Act s 60 要求 agent 持牌且有书面、由客户或合法授权人签署的有效 appointment to act，才能收取 commission。
