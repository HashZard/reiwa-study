Explain one key difference between a Comparative Market Analysis (CMA) and a formal valuation.

## Answer / 作答

### Answer in English / 英文答案草稿

A CMA is an agent’s evidence-based estimate of the likely market price using comparable properties. A formal valuation is completed by a qualified valuer and may be used for legal or financial purposes.

### 中文解析

CMA 是销售代表基于 comparable evidence 给出的市场意见；formal valuation 是 qualified valuer 提供的正式服务，适用高风险、法律或金融用途。
