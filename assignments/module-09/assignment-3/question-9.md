# Module 9 — Assignment 3 — Question 9

## Question / 题目 9

### English

What must be included in a written opinion of market price under the REBA Code?

Options include client agreement; agency costs; agent details; or the opinion, reasons and supporting evidence.

### 中文题意

选择 REBA Code Rule 26 对书面市场价格意见的内容要求。

## Answer / 作答

### Answer in English / 英文答案草稿

The stated opinion, the reasons for that opinion, and supporting sales or rental evidence if it is available.

### 中文解析

Rule 26 的三个字段必须同时覆盖，不能只给价格区间。
