## Answer / 作答

### Answer in English / 英文答案草稿

1. Current market conditions — Influences supply and demand, market values, and market sentiment.
2. Local demographics — Helps identify the target market for the property.
3. Economic, political and social factors — Impacts buyer confidence, affordability, and demand.
4. Local amenities and schools — Drives buyer or renter demand and affects desirability.

### 中文解析

四项按平台下拉选项一一对应。WB4 Section 3 解释了利率、政策、人口与经济周期如何影响需求；WB4 §2.6 说明 school zones、parks、transport 等影响 desirability。
