## Answer / 作答

### Answer in English / 英文答案草稿

1. “An agency gives you professional market research, compliant documents, marketing, buyer management and negotiation support. We also keep records, communicate offers and guide the sale through to settlement. This reduces your workload and risk.”

2. “Phantom Realty can inspect the property, prepare the written appraisal and CMA, recommend a method of sale, arrange photography and advertising, manage enquiries and home opens, follow up buyers, present every offer, negotiate under your instructions and coordinate the transaction.”

3. “The selling fee, marketing costs and other expenses will be explained and agreed in writing before you sign the Appointment to Act. Fees are negotiable. The agreement will show how the selling fee is calculated, the services included, the marketing budget and the maximum amounts payable.”

4. “We can use private treaty, auction, Expressions of Interest or a set-date sale. Private treaty lets buyers submit offers and terms for you to consider. Auction creates a fixed campaign and competitive bidding, but needs a reserve and auction costs. Expressions of Interest or a set-date sale asks buyers to submit their best offers by a deadline. I will recommend the method that best suits your timeframe, the property and current buyer demand.”

5. “I cannot guarantee an exact selling time. I will show you current days-on-market evidence and explain factors such as price, presentation, demand and the method of sale. We can plan the campaign around your travel date, but the final timing will depend on the market and the terms of an acceptable offer.”

6. “I understand that you do not want extra work before travelling. However, the air-conditioner and bore may affect buyer interest, price and disclosure. We can obtain repair quotes and compare the likely benefit. If you decide not to repair them, we must accurately record and disclose known material facts and market the property honestly.”

7. “I will agree a communication plan with you before the campaign starts. I suggest a short update after each inspection, an immediate phone call for any offer or urgent issue, and a full weekly email with enquiries, website activity, buyer feedback and next steps. I can also copy Shirley into updates if you both authorise this.”

### 中文解析

七项均使用 direct speech，并避免保证价格或销售时间。费用只说明必须书面、可协商及需列明的内容，不编造 Phantom Realty 未给出的实际收费。修理问题同时覆盖 client choice、price impact 与 material-fact disclosure。
