## Question / 题目 4

### English

Write three professional questions to Robert to identify material facts relating to both properties, including the apparent extension and old water bore.

## Answer / 作答

### Answer in English / 英文答案草稿

1. “Robert, was the extension at 7 Phillip Island Drive approved by the local council, and do you have the building plans, permits and final approvals?”
2. “Are there any known faults, leaks, safety concerns or repair history for the water bore, air-conditioner or other services at either property?”
3. “For 2/9 Storybrook Place, what renovations are planned, have strata approvals been obtained where required, and are you aware of any defects, levies, by-law issues or restrictions that should be disclosed to a future tenant?”

### 中文解析

三问分别覆盖 extension approval、设备/安全问题，以及 strata rental property 的 renovation、defects 和 restrictions。问题没有假设事实已经存在，而是要求 Robert 提供可核验资料。
