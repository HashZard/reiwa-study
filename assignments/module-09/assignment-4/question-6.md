## Answer / 作答

### Answer in English / 英文答案草稿

“Thank you for your enquiry. I cannot disclose the owner’s identity, private circumstances, motivation or any non-public property information without the client’s informed written consent. I can provide information that Robert has authorised for marketing. I also cannot arrange an immediate inspection without first obtaining Robert’s instructions and confirming an agreed time. Please send me the buyers’ names and contact details and the information they need. I will speak with Robert and come back to you with the approved details and an inspection time.

I am taking this approach because Rule 9 of the REBA Code requires confidential client information to be protected unless disclosure is required by law or the fully informed client agrees in writing. Rules 5, 7 and 10 also require me to act in the client’s best interests, be honest and follow the client’s reasonable instructions.”

### 中文解析

答复逐项覆盖 ownership、details、urgency 与 same-day inspection。可分享的只有客户已授权公开的 marketing information；看房须先取得指示并安排时间。
