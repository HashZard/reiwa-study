## Answer / 作答

### Answer in English / 英文答案草稿

1. “I understand why that sale looks important, Robert. I will verify it and keep it as market context. However, a comparable must be similar in size, age, condition, land area and location. Because that home is larger, newer and on a bigger block, its higher price cannot be applied directly to your property. I will explain the differences and give more weight to the closest like-for-like sales.”

2. “No, I cannot hide known problems with the air-conditioner or water bore. We must make reasonable efforts to verify material facts and promptly tell a buyer who may be affected and appears unaware. Hiding the issues could also be misleading. We can decide whether to repair them, obtain reports or sell in the current condition, but the information must be handled honestly.”

3. “I cannot recommend advertising at a deliberately misleading low price just to attract buyers. The advertised price must be authorised by you, but our marketing must also be honest and supported by the written appraisal. I suggest we use a realistic price or range based on the CMA and review the strategy if new evidence changes the market position.”

4. “No. A buyer should put the offer and all important terms in writing, usually in the Contract for Sale of Land or Strata Title by Offer and Acceptance. I must communicate every offer to you as soon as practicable. You can then accept it, reject it or make a written counter-offer after considering the price, finance, settlement date and other conditions.”

### 中文解析

第一段不忽略 nearby sale，但说明必须 adjust differences。第二和第三段明确不能按客户要求隐瞒或 bait advertise。第四段区分 buyer offer、agent communication duty 和 seller decision。
