# Do Not Call Register Act 2006 (Cth)

## Relevance to Module 9 Workbook 1

Workbook 1, section 1.6 and section 4.1, identifies the Do Not Call Register (DNCR) as a legal framework for prospecting. This Act regulates unsolicited telemarketing calls to Australian numbers registered on the Register. Calling-time rules are in the separate [Telecommunications (Telemarketing and Research Calls) Industry Standard 2017](cth-telecommunications-telemarketing-research-calls-industry-standard-2017.md).

## Section 11 — Unsolicited telemarketing calls must not be made to a number registered on the Do Not Call Register

Section 11(1) provides that a person must not make, or cause to be made, a telemarketing call to an Australian number if the number is registered on the Do Not Call Register and the call is not a designated telemarketing call.

The main relevant qualifications are:

- the prohibition does not apply where the relevant account-holder or their nominee consented to the call: s 11(2);
- a person relying on a Register-wash result may have an exception where the statutory conditions in s 11(3) are met, including the 30-day period specified there;
- reasonable precautions and due diligence may be relevant under s 11(5);
- s 11(1) and ancillary contraventions in s 11(7) are civil penalty provisions: s 11(8).
