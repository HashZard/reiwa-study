# Spam Act 2003 (Cth)

## Relevance to Module 9 Workbook 1

Workbook 1 section 4.1 names the Spam Act 2003 for prospecting messages such as email, SMS/MMS and instant messaging.

## Section 16 — Unsolicited commercial electronic messages must not be sent

Section 16(1) prohibits sending, or causing to be sent, a commercial electronic message with an Australian link unless it is a designated commercial electronic message. Consent of the relevant electronic account-holder is an exception under s 16(2), subject to the Act’s definitions and conditions. The civil-penalty status is stated in s 16(11).

## Section 17 — Accurate sender information

Section 17(1) requires a commercial electronic message with an Australian link to clearly and accurately identify the individual or organisation authorising it, and to include accurate contact information. That information must be reasonably likely to remain valid for at least 30 days after the message is sent, subject to the section and any regulatory conditions.

## Section 18 — Functional unsubscribe facility

Section 18(1) requires a qualifying commercial electronic message to contain a clear and conspicuous unsubscribe statement and an electronic address capable of receiving unsubscribe messages. The address must be reasonably likely to work for at least 30 days after sending, subject to the full statutory conditions.

> Module 9’s statement that unsubscribe requests require immediate action is course guidance. Do not substitute that wording for the statutory conditions or timeframes without checking the Act and any applicable regulations.
