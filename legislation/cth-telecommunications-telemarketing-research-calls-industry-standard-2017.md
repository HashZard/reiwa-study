# Telecommunications (Telemarketing and Research Calls) Industry Standard 2017 (Cth)

## Relevance to Module 9 Workbook 1

Workbook 1 section 4.1 gives prospecting cold-calling time limits. Those limits are governed by this Industry Standard, not by the Do Not Call Register Act alone.

## Section 8 — Prohibited calling times

For a call that is not a research call, s 8(1) prohibits making or attempting the call:

- before 9 am or after 8 pm on a weekday;
- before 9 am or after 5 pm on Saturday; or
- on Sunday.

Section 8(3) also prohibits calls at any time on the specified national public holidays and substitute weekday holidays. The applicable time is the time at the usual residential address of the relevant account-holder: s 8(4).

Express advance consent to a particular call can be an exception only if the conditions in s 8(5) are met.

## Section 9 — Information during telemarketing calls other than research calls

Section 9 requires specified information to be given as soon as the call starts, including the caller’s given name (unless the call is solely recorded or synthetic voice), the relevant employer/organisation identity, and the purpose of the call. Read the official PDF for the complete requirements and exceptions.

> The Workbook statement “Sundays or Public Holidays: Never” is course material. For an answer requiring current law, check the exact scope of s 8 and any applicable State or Territory rule on the official source.
