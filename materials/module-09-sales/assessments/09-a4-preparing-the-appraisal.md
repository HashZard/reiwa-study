## Overview and Instructions

This assessment simulates a full appraisal process for two Fantasy Glen properties. It requires market research, CMAs, forms and professional client communication, including confidentiality, material-fact disclosure and REBA Code compliance.

## Setting the scene

Successful contact
Your prospecting efforts have paid off — you’ve connected with Robert Cagiva, a long-time property owner in Fantasy Glen who’s preparing for retirement and planning to travel with his wife, Shirley.​ 
Robert owns several local properties and has asked you to prepare appraisals for two of them:​ 
Property A: 7 Phillip Island Drive – a residential home he plans to sell before travelling.
Property B: 2/9 Storybrook Place – a strata-titled unit he intends to lease after completing minor renovations. 

You’ll now begin the appraisal process using available property details, local market research, and agency tools such as RP Data and REIWA.​
